# My First Flight

My family and I went to the airport this summer.
I was happy because it was my first time to fly by plane.
At the airport, we saw many big planes.
My father said planes can travel fast and take us to different countries.
We walked to the gate and waited.
I watched a plane arrive.
It was amazing!
Soon, it was time to get on our plane.
When the plane started to fly, I looked out.
The cars and houses looked so small!
My mother said people study for many years to learn how to fly planes.
It was a wonderful trip, and I hope to fly by plane soon.

- **Estimated Reading Time**: 2 minutes
