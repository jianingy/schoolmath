# The Lost Pupil

Emma was a young pupil in Grade Five who loved animals more than anything.
Every day after school, she would visit the zoo near her neighbourhood to watch the animals.
Her favourite was a clever monkey named Thomas who could catch peanuts in the air.
One Saturday morning, Emma arrived at the zoo early and found something amazing.
A small penguin had escaped from its enclosure and was walking on the sidewalk.
The penguin looked scared and lost.
Emma felt worried about the poor bird.
She wanted to help, but she didn't know what to do.
"Be careful!" shouted the zookeeper from across the street.
Emma stayed calm and spoke softly to the penguin.
She remembered her teacher once said animals can hear your kind voice.
The penguin stopped and looked at her with curious eyes.
Emma slowly walked towards it, keeping a safe distance.
She took out her sandwich from her schoolbag and placed it on the ground.
The hungry penguin came closer and started to eat.
While the penguin was busy eating, the zookeeper arrived with a warm blanket.
Together, they carefully brought the penguin back to its home.
The zookeeper was very proud of Emma's brave actions.
"You were really helpful today," he said with a big smile.
As a thank you, he gave Emma a special volunteer card.
Now Emma could visit the zoo for free and help take care of the animals.
She learned that being patient and kind can solve many problems.
From that day on, Emma spent every weekend as a volunteer at the zoo.
She helped feed the animals, clean their rooms, and teach visitors about wildlife.
Her dream was to become a scientist who studies animals in the future.
